# Material imported definitions

The geometric harmonic correction uses actual endpoint-fixed homotopies and the real span of null-loop word chains. The obstruction subspace is NullSpan intersect old Relations-orthogonal. Only E is finite; vertices and rule witnesses may be infinite, empty or disconnected.

The actual norm on ChainH1 is inherited from the canonical quotient of the cycle subspace by ChainBoundaries. It is the coset infimum of the existing cycle norm. Subtype norms are restrictions. No kernel norm is introduced by transport. Pinned quotientEquivOrthogonal proves the existing quotient norm agrees with the orthogonal representative norm. The package restricts the actual quotient map to the geometric kernel and proves bijectivity and norm preservation.

Classical projection/Hodge and closest-point theory are attributed. No ordinary-product isometry, ambient singular/deRham comparison, integral result, analytic/Kahler theorem, numerical algorithm or novelty/acceptance is claimed. Finsupp.sum and the additive quotient seminorm have generated source producers; their supporting producers and actual additive equations are retained without inventing ordinary definition headers.

    NormedSpace 𝕜 E, Inner 𝕜 E where
  /-- The inner product induces the norm. -/

  /-- The inner product is *Hermitian*, taking the `conj` swaps the arguments. -/

  smul_left : ∀ x y r, inner (r • x) y = conj r * inner x y
