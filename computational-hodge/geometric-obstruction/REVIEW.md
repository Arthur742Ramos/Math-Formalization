# Review contract and evidence boundaries

The selected result is a geometric harmonic correction and metric obstruction
theorem for the user's actual continuous computational-path model. It is not a
new generic orthogonal projection theorem or an analytic Hodge theorem.

Independent internal AI scope review required the actual quotient-norm kernel
isometry as a core result. The map is literally `b ↦ [b]P`; its inverse is proved
using null-span representatives and subtraction of the old relation projection.
No norm is redefined to manufacture an isometry. The all-competitors error
identity and unique closest correction use existing finite Euclidean norms.
No ordinary-product isometry or preservation of arbitrary old classes is claimed.

Before verified handoff or merge, review the exact source and preserve separate
source-bound evidence for:

1. All statements, assumptions and quantifiers, including arbitrary vertices
   and rules and the empty-generator case; the actual path and norm meanings;
   complete source mapping and meaningful nonduplicate scope.
2. The complete twelve-module proof surface, both entrypoints, all 47 declaration
   kinds/types and selected definition values, strict dependency comparison,
   three mutation rejection controls and random-module alias comparison.
3. Clean exact-toolchain builds, semantic bridges, transitive axiom audits,
   pinned source/dependency provenance, metadata and all required root checks.
4. The unchanged pinned full canonical CLI and independent kernel replay,
   original controls and confinement/capacity requirements. A component check
   does not substitute for the full exact-public-commit CLI.
5. The unchanged canonical renderer, sanitization/proof preservation, actual
   browser inspection of representative complete proofs, definition links and
   tactic states, and an authenticated exact artifact binding.
6. The exact source archive, final release-packet guard, truthful public prose,
   independent final evidence review and ordinary repository-protected merge.
   Carry evidence across a merge only after actual whole-tree equality.

The package makes no time-dependent claim that a particular gate is globally
pending or complete. Completed results identify their actual tested commit,
tool/config/source hashes, times and immutable receipts outside the frozen
source. Prior passes are historical evidence, not fresh checks on changed bytes.

Generic projection and quotient-isometry results are credited to pinned Mathlib;
classical finite Hodge/least-squares context is credited to Jiang–Lim–Yao–Ye.
The geometric model and predecessor proof subsets are explicitly attributed.
Internal AI review is distinct from human review and external peer review.
Mechanical/source checks do not establish novelty, editorial acceptance,
registration, or analytic/singular/de Rham comparison. No registry action is
authorized by this document.
