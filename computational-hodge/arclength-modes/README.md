# All-frequency arclength modes and computational loops

For every positive C1 length density on the real period-one circle and every natural `n > 0`, construct actual C2 cosine and sine coefficients by pulling the flat modes back along the normalized real arclength coordinate. Both have eigenvalue `(2πn/m)²`, where `m` is the positive density mean. Their shifts strictly increase with `n`, and the two coefficients are linearly independent. This gives at least two independent modes at each of infinitely many distinct shifts.

The actual endpoint-indexed binary computational traces retain composition, reversal, realization and covering lifts. Both mode values along every actual based trace are the cosine or sine of its constructed lifted phase. The phase-loop winding equals `n * count p`. The actual projected-curve integrands are proved interval integrable; the weighted raw and finite harmonic-cochain periods of each mode vanish on every based trace. Nevertheless each nonzero mode is outside the range of the corresponding shifted Laplacian over all actual C2 competitors.

The result concerns a real scalar circle.
