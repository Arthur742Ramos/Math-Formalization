# Scope and fidelity review

Independent internal AI scope review inspected the actual computational-path
definitions and PR59–64, including existing circle/torus classification. It
selected nonconstant actual continuous circle coefficients, a quotient-descended
potential and genuine ordinary integrals as a substantive analytic bridge.
A constant-covector FTC wrapper was considered insufficient. No new classical
circle classification or full manifold Hodge result is claimed.

Mathematical authoring review found no blocker in the actual mean-subtracted
primitive, C1 regularity, periodic quotient descent, analytic exactness,
guarded harmonicity or uniqueness. Chain-rule FTC works for the globally C1
real lift without requiring monotonicity. Actual covering-lift uniqueness
identifies its endpoint with winding, and actual winding integrality and
injectivity prove every ambient class has a C1 representative. These are
proved obligations, not assumptions replacing the analytic bridge.

The retained primitive is the genuine unit-winding circle loop. Rooted-loop
reconstruction produces the finite harmonic cochain, whose actual signed
periods equal ordinary integrals on every C1 representative of a trace class.
The unit generator proves uniqueness nonvacuously. The mean map is surjective
onto the actual finite harmonic subspace and its fibers are exactly analytic
exact differences. Finite Exact is zero and is not identified with analytic
ExactCoefficient. Review also checked the actual integral orthogonality and
energy Pythagoras identity, including integrability of squared coefficients.

The upstream and predecessor source mappings preserve all retained complete
proofs, with explicit import/namespace adaptations and immutable byte ranges.
Unused torus product and rewrite-closure declarations are omitted; the package
does not claim to retain them. Both canonical surfaces preserve every new
declaration and proof. Material dependency evidence exposes actual Bochner
integration, its default-zero convention, actual derivative totalization,
C1 characterization, periodic quotient lifting, FTC and circle covering.
Regularity/integrability are proved, and independent Challenge/Solution
semantic modules must compile. Underlying Lebesgue/L1/little-o/Taylor

Authoring review is distinct from final immutable-source and evidence review.
Native/comparator/negative-control/kernel/canonical/render/browser/packet
gates remain separately source-bound. Shared Challenge/Solution proofs and
AI proof contribution are disclosed; no human or external peer review is
invented. Source changes require affected checks again.

The scope is a flat one-dimensional coefficient model. Direct integration of
raw cornered computational traces, manifold differential forms, singular or
de Rham comparison, higher-dimensional/Kähler Hodge theory, Laplacians,
L2 quotient norms and numerical computation remain outside this package.
Existing registry intakes and provider repositories remain untouched.
