# Positive-resolvent calculus and computational loops

For a positive C1 density on the real period-one circle, the actual C2 solution operator Rκ of the weighted Laplacian plus κ is a continuous operator on C0 for each κ>0. The proofs establish the two-parameter resolvent identity, commutation including equal parameters, an operator-norm bound and difference estimate, and uniform control on every positive half-line. The positive-shift family has operator-norm derivative −Rκ².

The same construction differentiates the literal weighted raw integral and finite harmonic-cochain period along every actual based computational loop `Trace 0 0`. These statements use the user's indexed binary composition and reversal, realized circle curves, covering lifts, and AC/FTC integration through all corners. They do not identify a general de Rham class.

The derivative is in the C0 operator norm, not a C2 or Hilbert operator norm. The total family is zero outside positive shifts; no regularity there is asserted. No general manifold or Kähler Hodge theorem, complex-analytic or spectral result, arbitrary open-path result, numerical solver or novelty is claimed. Classical resolvent identity context: Kato, *Perturbation Theory for Linear Operators*, III §6.1, pp. 173–174, with spectral parameter ζ=−κ.
