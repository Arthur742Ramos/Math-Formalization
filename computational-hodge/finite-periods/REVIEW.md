# Scope and source review

An independent internal AI review assessed the mathematical design and the
actual source. It did not infer human proof contributions, human mathematical
review, external peer review, novelty or registry acceptance.

The model is the endpoint-changing geometric trace calculus in the attributed
upstream port. All fourteen scoped rewrite constructors and all named-rule
witnesses are represented. It does not replace that calculus with
proof-irrelevant equality metadata, a length statistic or a freely assumed
cohomology/harmonic equivalence.

The review checked the actual signed period recursion, endpoint telescoping,
`d1(d0 f)=0`, and closure under every scoped rewrite constructor. The degree-one
space is finite dimensional because the generator type is finite. Neither the
point space nor the rule family is required to be finite. Orthogonal projection
exists for the actual exact subspace by inferred finite-dimensional
completeness; no representative-existence or Hodge-decomposition hypothesis is
introduced.

Boundaries are restricted to closed cochains before forming the quotient.
The representative is constructed as the original vector minus its exact
projection. Uniqueness uses the zero intersection of a subspace and its
orthogonal complement. The quotient equivalence and both independent period
descents are explicit constructions. Componentwise trace potentials establish
period separation across all basepoints without a connectivity assumption.

The parallel-edge model supplies a nonzero harmonic cochain with period two,
an actual ambient null-homotopy, and a named-rewrite control that kills the
harmonic space. The first fact is about presentation cohomology; the second
shows why an ambient de Rham identification would be false without additional
results. The example's named scoped topology is the same discrete finite-step
topology as the original local instance, with stable exported identity.

The upstream definitions and MIT license are retained literally. Import,
module-visibility and namespace-closure adaptations are separately recorded.
The material Mathlib inventory distinguishes real abbreviations from
definitions, expands the WithLp wrapper, records the proved quotient-relation
interpretation and retains the finite-dimensional completeness/projection
dependencies. Separate semantic modules check these interfaces through
Challenge and Solution independently. Static provenance checks do not replace
their compilation or establish inventory completeness by themselves.

All modular declarations and complete proofs are copied into both standalone
interfaces. Their common implementation and source transformation are disclosed
in `surface-manifest.json`; they are not independent proofs. Required kinds,
literal definition values, malformed-statement/closure/value controls, source
closure and random-module-alias behavior are checked by the native interface
checker. These writable local checks do not claim protected canonical execution.

Exact reviewed hashes, actual compilation, independent kernels, protected
comparison, renderer bounds, visual review and final packet decisions belong

affected checks and independent review again. No global completed verification
