## Mathematical antecedents

The grounded fixed-point equivalence is an established theorem of Bogaerts,
Vennekens and Denecker, *Grounded fixpoints and their applications in knowledge
representation*, Artificial Intelligence 224 (2015), 51–71, Proposition 3.10
and the powerset discussion/Proposition 3.5; DOI 10.1016/j.artint.2015.03.006.
Its formal specialization is not claimed as a new general theorem.

Infinite copying with two solutions and well-foundedness sufficiency are
explicit antecedents in Peters and Halpern, *Causal Modeling With Infinitely
Many Variables*, arXiv:2112.09171 (2021), pp. 1–2. Finite exact-interaction-graph
fixed-point constructions are studied by Aracena, Richard and Salinas,
SIAM J.Discrete Math. 31(3) (2017), 1702–1725,DOI 10.1137/16M1060868.
Richard's finite subnetwork uniqueness characterization is in Theoretical
Computer Science 583 (2015), 1–26,DOI 10.1016/j.tcs.2015.03.038.

Formal verification checks stated definitions and hypotheses. It does not
certify philosophical adequacy, priority, actual causation or publication merit.
