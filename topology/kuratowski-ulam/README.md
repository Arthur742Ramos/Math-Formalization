# Kuratowski–Ulam category theorem for countable π-base spaces

For arbitrary topological spaces X,Y, a countable π-base on Y is enough for the
classical section theorem: a nowhere dense set in X × Y has nowhere dense
vertical sections outside a **meagre** exceptional set in X. A meagre set has
meagre sections for residually many x, and a residual set has residual sections.
These forward results require neither factor to be Baire, Hausdorff or nonempty.
Residual sets need not be dense in a non-Baire space.

For a set with the **property of Baire**, if both factors are Baire, meagreness
and residuality are equivalent to their generic section counterparts. The product
of two Baire spaces is Baire when the second has a countable π-base. These are
proved companions; no unrestricted productivity theorem for Baire spaces is claimed.

A π-base is a family of nonempty open sets refining every nonempty open set.
It need not cover points or be a topological basis. Countability is expressed by
canonical set countability; the family may be empty when Y is empty. Second
countability implies this condition. Independent type universes and empty
products are allowed; no hidden global nonemptiness assumption is used.

## Proof and research source

Kucharski–Plewik, *Game Approach to Universally Kuratowski-Ulam Spaces*,
[arXiv:math/0605469v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0605469v1), printed pp.1–2,
recalls the exact classical nowhere-dense theorem and meagre lifting.
Kalemba–Kucharski, [arXiv:1202.2056v3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1202.2056v3),
printed p.3, independently recalls it in a research setting of category and
open-open games. Their full-text PDF hashes are in formalization.yaml.
The original Kuratowski–Ulam source was not consulted. No novelty, priority or
first-formalization claim is made. Stronger game, hyperspace and universality
characterizations in those papers are outside this package.

The proof constructs, for each member V of a countable π-base, the open dense
projection of U ∩ (X × V), where U is a dense open product set. A countable
intersection gives generic dense sections. Closed supersets give the
nowhere-dense form; generated residual filters give meagre/residual lifting.
The converse uses Mathlib’s open-modulo-meagre representation and a nonempty
rectangle; the two factor Baire hypotheses are used explicitly. The product
Baire proof uses the forward theorem without assuming the product is Baire.
These bridges are proved here. Canonical product topology, category notions,
countability, residual filters and open approximation are credited to Mathlib.

`BaireMeasurableSet` means the property of Baire here, not the Baire sigma-algebra
generated by continuous functions. The papers use differing uK-U/uK-U-star
terminology, so metadata uses literal section properties. No measure-theoretic
Fubini theorem, cardinal-invariant bound or uncountable π-base extension is asserted.
