# Internal review scope

Before implementation, two independent internal AI reviewers assessed the
primary theorem, explicit hypotheses, substantive Mathlib gap and bounded
campaign overlap. A separate mathematical implementation review checked the
infinite partition, bijective indexing, limit homeomorphism and converse.
Retained exact-source review receipts are outside the frozen source and must
be bound by the final release packet. The semantic inventory is independently
reviewed separately from its source-consistency validator and Lean bridges.

These are internal AI reviews, not human mathematical or external peer review.
They neither promise novelty nor establish registry acceptance. The exact
final packet is reviewed after evidence is available; changes require affected
checks and fresh file bindings. Global/private overlap limits remain explicit.
