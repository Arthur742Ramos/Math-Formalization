the exact bytes and scope recorded in those receipts. Internal AI review is
not human review, external peer review or a Palomar editorial decision.

browser inspection and registration have separate source-bound receipts. The

no root overlay can excuse a contradiction within the proposed source.

verification. Any final verified packet needs the original exact-source full
report and regular-file source archive accepted by the root release guard.
