# Review scope and limits

Independent internal mathematical/source review checks the arbitrary-topology
Baire-times-alpha-favorable statement against the consulted primary recall,
full opponent-history model, coherent disjoint-rectangle branch, fixed-region
fiber quantifiers, empty factors and complete-metric closed-trap argument.
Independent implementation review checks Challenge/Solution separation,
fixed dependency values, all selected contracts, transitive axioms, exact
pins/material source provenance, actual verification artifacts and final
packet bytes. Internal AI review is not human or external peer review.

The original White1975 proof was not consulted. No strong-Choquet
characterization, arbitrary-product result, novelty or priority is claimed.
A fixed V produces its own G; dense entire fibers are not asserted.
Complete metric balls need not be compact. Empty-space alpha-favorability
is explicitly vacuous and does not invent a legal play.

Completed reviews and verification stages must have immutable exact-source
receipts outside the frozen source. Compiler, protected independent kernels,
rendering, browser runtime, editorial review and registration have separate
scopes. No earlier no-intake observation excludes later user activity.
