# Standard definitions at the pinned Mathlib revision

All statement notions are imported from canonical Mathlib. There are no new
selected definitions and no project-specific dependency in Challenge.

## Set.diagonal

The literal set of pairs with equal coordinates. [Pinned source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/065356127b1dc0016f66b7283ce0ce2c4055aa55/Mathlib/Data/Set/Operations.lean#L258).

## IsGδ

Existence of a countable family of open sets whose intersection is the set. [Pinned source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/065356127b1dc0016f66b7283ce0ce2c4055aa55/Mathlib/Topology/GDelta/Basic.lean#L63).

## nhdsSet

The supremum of the point-neighborhood filters over the set. [Pinned source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/065356127b1dc0016f66b7283ce0ce2c4055aa55/Mathlib/Topology/Defs/Filter.lean#L161).

## IsCompact and CompactSpace

Filter-cluster-point compactness, with CompactSpace asserting compactness of univ. [Pinned source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/065356127b1dc0016f66b7283ce0ce2c4055aa55/Mathlib/Topology/Defs/Filter.lean#L290).

## NormalSpace

Disjoint closed sets admit separated neighborhoods; no T1 condition is bundled. [Pinned source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/065356127b1dc0016f66b7283ce0ce2c4055aa55/Mathlib/Topology/Separation/Regular.lean#L498).

## T2Space

Distinct points admit disjoint open neighborhoods. [Pinned source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/065356127b1dc0016f66b7283ce0ce2c4055aa55/Mathlib/Topology/Separation/Hausdorff.lean#L85).

## Filter.HasBasis

Membership iff containing one indicated basis set; this asserts cofinality. [Pinned source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/065356127b1dc0016f66b7283ce0ce2c4055aa55/Mathlib/Order/Filter/Bases/Basic.lean#L209).

## Filter.IsCountablyGenerated

The filter is generated by a countable family. [Pinned source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/065356127b1dc0016f66b7283ce0ce2c4055aa55/Mathlib/Order/Filter/CountablyGenerated.lean#L30).

## TopologicalSpace.MetrizableSpace

A compatible countably generated uniformity and T0 separation. [Pinned source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/065356127b1dc0016f66b7283ce0ce2c4055aa55/Mathlib/Topology/Metrizable/Basic.lean#L125).

## MetricSpace

An ordinary real-valued pseudometric with distance-zero separation. [Pinned source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/065356127b1dc0016f66b7283ce0ce2c4055aa55/Mathlib/Topology/MetricSpace/Defs.lean#L70).

## uniformSpaceOfCompactR1

Canonical compact compatible uniformity, defined to be neighborhoods of the diagonal. [Pinned source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/065356127b1dc0016f66b7283ce0ce2c4055aa55/Mathlib/Topology/UniformSpace/OfCompactT2.lean#L45).

The product topology on `X × X` is the standard induced-product instance.
`ChallengeTests.lean` states the main equivalence with that instance explicitly.
The metric witness equates its induced topology to the original `t`; it does
not assert a replacement topology. `SemanticTests.lean` includes finite empty
intersections and empty/full set cases. Full file hashes for the upstream
sources are recorded in `verification/definitions.json`.
